Thesis topics

Open thesis topics at the Chair of Data Science

Bachelor's and Master's thesis topics grouped around open web search and OUR²S ranking, URL risk classification, hierarchical classification for the Open Web Index, multi-objective LLM alignment, and API design for AI agents.

Each topic ships with a scoped 1–2 week entry task. The open OUR²S / YOARS theme accepts self-defined research questions via a one-page proposal.

Browse open thesis topics

How to apply

The thesis process runs through the Oberseminar: topic selection, entry task, exposé, and registration. Registration is binding and starts the official six-month working period for Master's theses.

Prof. Granitzer holds a weekly Q&A slot on Monday 14:00–16:00, by prior registration through the chair's secretariat.
