Erasmus Exchange IT:U Linz and University of Passau

Information page for an Erasmus exchange between IT:U Linz and the University of Passau.

TypeErasmus exchange
InstitutionsIT:U Linz · University of Passau
Statusin preparation
ContactStephanie Pauli, University of Passau; Kanishka Ghosh Dastidar, IT:U

The Erasmus exchange between the Interdisciplinary Transformation University - IT:U Linz and the University of Passau connects two complementary study environments in computer science, data science, and digital transformation.

For students from Passau, the exchange offers access to IT:U's project-based learning environment, where interdisciplinary teams work on open-ended problems and connect technical work with societal contexts.

For students from IT:U, the exchange offers access to Passau's deep theoretical curriculum, including courses in data science, network science, machine learning, multimedia databases, and text mining.
